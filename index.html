Engineer by degree, automator by instinct

I'm a results-oriented BIM Engineer with over six years across civil engineering and Building Information Modeling — from site supervision early in my career to leading modeling and automation for international projects today.

My sweet spot is where modeling meets code: visualization and project execution in Revit, AutoCAD, Navisworks, Enscape and Autodesk Construction Cloud, supercharged by Dynamo, Python and the Revit API.
